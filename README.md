# El Anaka Ascend

Build a modern, fully responsive, and high-conversion web application for "El Anaka Academy," a prestigious and long-standing vocational training center (established in 1972) in Sfax, Tunisia. The school specializes in practical, state-certified training programs across three main pillars: Culinary Arts & Pastry, Beauty & Aesthetics, and Fashion/Clothing Design.

### Design System & Aesthetic (Freestyle High-End Modern)

- **Vibe:** Professional, trustworthy, warm, and sophisticated. It should blend the credibility of a 50+ year legacy institution with a sleek, modern digital school experience.

- **Color Palette:** 

  - Primary Base: Deep Marine Blue (#0F1E36 or similar rich navy) for a sense of trust and authority.

  - Accent/Highlight: Luxurious Yellow Gold (#D4AF37 or #E5C158) for CTAs, badges, and subtle border highlights.

  - Backgrounds: Clean crisp whites and soft off-whites/light grays with generous whitespace.

- **Typography:** Clean, highly readable sans-serif fonts (like Inter or Outfit) with bold headers and smooth paragraph spacing.

- **Responsiveness:** 100% mobile-first design. The layouts, navigation, and forms must scale flawlessly from mobile screens to ultrawide monitors.

### Page Architecture & Features to Build

1. **Top Navigation Bar (Sticky)**

   - Logo: "EL ANAKA" with a subtle badge noting "Since 1972".

   - Links: Home, Departments, About Us, Student Space, News, Contact.

   - Language Switcher dropdown (FR / AR / EN).

   - Prominent Gold CTA Button: "Pre-Register Now" or "S'inscrire".

2. **Hero Section**

   - Headline: Empowering Future Professionals Since 1972 (or in French: Formez-vous aux métiers d'avenir).

   - Subheadline: State-certified vocational training in Culinary Arts, Aesthetics, and Fashion Design in Sfax.

   - Dual CTAs: "Explore Programs" (Marine Blue) and "Contact Admissions" (Gold outline/fill).

   - High-quality layout incorporating placeholder imagery or modern abstract geometric/educational grids reflecting hands-on trades.

3. **Departments / Programs Grid (The Core Offering)**

   - Interactive cards or tabs for the 3 main departments with gorgeous hover effects:

     - 🍳 **Culinary Arts & Pâtisserie:** Kitchen technician, European & Tunisian pastries, baking.

     - 💄 **Beauty & Aesthetics:** Hairdressing (men/women), advanced skincare, beautician training.

     - ✂️ **Fashion & Clothing:** Stylist-modéliste, cutting, and sewing.

   - Each card should feature key highlights (e.g., "Rapid training available", "State-certified") and a "View Curriculum" link.

4. **Why Choose El Anaka? (Trust Section)**

   - Grid layout showcasing stats and values:

     - 50+ Years of Excellence

     - State Pedagogical Control (Diplômes agréés par l'État)

     - Hands-on Practical Training

     - Professional Career Support

5. **Student Space & Registration Modal/Page**

   - A streamlined, easy-to-use digital inquiry/pre-registration form where prospective students can select their desired course, input their contact details, and submit an inquiry.

6. **Footer**

   - Headquarters info: Route de Tunis km. 0.5 Immeuble COTUB - 3002 Sfax, Tunisia.

   - Phone numbers, email, social media links, and a clean copyright notice.

Please use modern component architecture, smooth transitions, and ensure the UI looks polished and ready for production. this is there current website https://elanaka.com/
